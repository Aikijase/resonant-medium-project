\begin{abstract}
We propose the \emph{Echo Equation}, a finite-memory (non-Markovian) evolution law for the cosmological expansion. In the limit of vanishing memory the model reduces to $\Lambda$CDM, while finite memory generically imprints small, phase-coherent modulations in late-time growth and lensing. We present a minimal parameterization $(\tau,\kappa,\omega_0,Q)$, show how it maps to background and linear-growth observables, and illustrate data-compatibility on representative BAO+SN+CMB-lensing combinations at goodness-of-fit comparable to $\Lambda$CDM. The key discriminants are (i) an echo pattern in $f\sigma_8(z)$ with characteristic $\Delta z \simeq \pi/\omega_0$ and (ii) a correlated residual in galaxy–CMB-lensing cross-correlations. Both signatures are falsifiable with near-term data.
\end{abstract}
