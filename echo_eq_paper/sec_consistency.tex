\section{Theoretical consistency}
\label{sec:consistency}

\paragraph{Recovery of $\Lambda$CDM.} In the limit $\tau\!\to\!0$ and $\kappa\!\to\!0$, the kernel vanishes and the background reduces to the standard expansion law. Early-time physics is left untouched by imposing $K(\Delta t)\!\approx\!0$ for the relevant high-$z$ window.

\paragraph{Stability and causality.} With $\tau>0$, $Q>0$, and a causal kernel $K(\Delta t\!<\!0)=0$, the linear response remains bounded for small memory amplitudes. Resonant amplification is limited by damping and quality factor.

\paragraph{Parameter identifiability.} The combination $(\omega_0, Q)$ is most tightly constrained by the phase and sharpness of the residual; $(\tau,\kappa)$ affect amplitude and background tilt. Orthogonality of these imprints enables cross-checks across probes.
