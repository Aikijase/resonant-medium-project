# GLOSSARY (quick)

- **BAO** — Baryon Acoustic Oscillations; distances reported as `D_M/r_d` and `D_H/r_d` per redshift.
- **Pantheon+** — Type Ia supernova dataset; *standardized magnitudes* `m_b_corr` plus a full STAT+SYS covariance.
- **Alpha basis** — Fit distance scalings relative to a fiducial cosmology (your driver supports this mode).
- **SPD** — Symmetric Positive Definite; required for Cholesky decomposition of covariance matrices.
- **Eigenvalue floor** — Replace very small/negative eigenvalues with a small positive *floor* to stabilize inversions.
- **AIC/BIC/AICc** — Information criteria; lower is better (penalize extra parameters).
